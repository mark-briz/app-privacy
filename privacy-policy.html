1. Information we collect

1.1 Account and authentication

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (handled by our authentication provider; we do not store your plaintext password)
  • Name or display name you provide
  • Profile photo URL if provided by a sign-in provider (for example, Google)
  • Authentication session tokens needed to keep you signed in

Sign-in options may include email/password and Google sign-in.

1.2 Profile information

You may provide:

  • First name and last name
  • Height
  • Weight goal
  • Appearance/theme preference

1.3 Health and fitness data you enter

You may enter and store:

  • Body weight readings (date, time, weight)
  • Blood pressure readings (date, time, systolic, diastolic, heart rate)
  • Workout plans and machines
  • Workout sessions and set details (date, plan, machine, weight used, repetitions)

This information is user-entered. The app does not access your phone’s Health/Google Fit sensors unless you later enable a feature that does so (none at the time of this policy).

1.4 Information stored on your device

The app may store on your device:

  • Cached copies of your tracking data
  • Theme preference
  • “Remember user” preference and saved email (never your password), if you choose that option
  • Authentication session information needed to restore your login

1.5 Technical information

Our service providers may automatically process limited technical data such as IP address, device/browser type, and request timestamps when you authenticate or sync data over the network. We do not operate a separate advertising analytics SDK in the app at the time of this policy.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in
  • Save and display your profile, preferences, and tracking history
  • Sync selected data to our backend so it is available when you use the app
  • Calculate in-app insights you request (for example, BMI from weight and height you provide)
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the app
  • Respond to support or privacy requests

We do not sell your personal information.
We do not use your health data for advertising.

3. Where your information is stored

  • On your device: Local storage is used for app preferences and a local copy of tracking data.
  • In the cloud: Account data and synced fitness/health records are stored with our backend provider, Supabase (database and authentication), subject to row-level access controls intended to limit access to your own account data.
  • Sign-in providers: If you use Google sign-in, Google processes authentication information according to Google’s policies.

5. Data retention

  • We retain account and synced data while your account is active.
  • Local data remains on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or delete entries in the app.
  • If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify personal data we control within a reasonable period, except where we must retain information for legal, security, or dispute-resolution reasons.
